The Sun We Carry

In the Keeping

MANISH KUMAR

Set across the four days of Chhath, The Sun We Carry is a warm, reflective work of literary fiction about memory, identity, tradition and the meaning of belonging.

Rooted in the cultural landscape of Bihar yet written for readers everywhere, it looks beyond the festival itself to the quieter ways culture stays with us through family, stories, familiar habits and the things we inherit without noticing.
